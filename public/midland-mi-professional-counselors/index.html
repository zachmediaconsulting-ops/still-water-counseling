Clinical Director

Carol Boothroyd

Licensed Professional Counselor, CSAT (Certified Sexual Addiction Therapist), Certified EMDR Therapist & Consultant, EFT (Emotionally Focused Therapy) Trained, IFS (Internal Family Systems) Level 1 Trained

I find that some of the biggest barriers to healthy living are the day-to-day effects of unresolved trauma. Considering that trauma involves any stressful event that is prolonged, overwhelming, or unpredictable, we all deal with trauma to some degree. As a trauma therapist, I journey with my clients as they work through their darkest moments and discover the beauty of who God designed them to be.

I specialize in working with partners and spouses of individuals struggling with addiction to pornography or other sexual behaviors. I help the partner establish healthy boundaries, deal with the trauma of the spouse’s addiction, and develop a plan for moving forward.

I lead an ongoing Sexual Addiction Partners’ Group that is lively, vulnerable, and real. I am also trained as an Emotionally Focused Therapy (EFT) couples therapist and help couples work toward greater emotional connection and intimacy. It is a wonderful thing to see couples move from being “roommates” to having a vulnerable and intimate connection.

My educational background includes a BA in Nursing from the University of Michigan and a Master’s in Professional Counseling from Liberty University. I spent 10 years working for Cru, a non-denominational Christian organization, and several years working as an RN on an acute care inpatient psychiatric unit. Most importantly, I’m the proud mother of four adult children, from whom I’ve learned some of life’s most valuable lessons.

Rob Boothroyd

Licensed Professional Counselor, CSAT (Certified Sexual Addiction Therapist), EMDR Trained, EFT Trained, IFS Level 1 Trained

Today, many people struggle with sexually compulsive behaviors. They often tell themselves that it is normal, while being secretive about what they are doing, and they are unable to stop even when they sincerely try. I am a Certified Sex Addiction Therapist (CSAT), and I completed this extensive training to help people find freedom from compulsive sexual practices.

Freedom is possible. Because many of my clients are married, I use EFT (Emotionally Focused Therapy) to help them heal the relational wounds caused by the addiction. Additionally, because many people have unresolved trauma, I have trained in EMDR to help them deal with the wounds of the past.

I earned a Bachelor of Arts degree from the University of Delaware and a Professional Counseling degree from Liberty University. My working experience includes 10 years as a full-time staff member of Campus Crusade for Christ (Cru) and 20 years in the staffing industry.

Libbee Kelingos

Licensed Professional Counselor, EMDR Trained, EFT (Emotionally Focused Therapy) Trained, IFS (Internal Family Systems) Level 1 Trained

It takes courage to share your heart, and I consider it an honor to create a space where you feel valued, safe, and accepted for who you are and where you are in your life.

I express empathy, non-judgment, and compassion to gently help you find healing, wholeness, purpose, and meaning, as well as gain insight and learn skills to help you develop your potential and achieve your goals. Whether or not you choose to incorporate faith, I take a holistic therapeutic approach to collaboratively integrate the spiritual, emotional, physical, and mental aspects of who you are.

Widowed in 2004, I became a single mother of two young children, who are now young adults. My experience includes being a Stephen Minister, a care coordinator at my church, a suicide support group facilitator, and a paraprofessional working with adolescents with special needs. I obtained my Bachelor of Arts in Communication from Michigan State University and my Master of Arts in Counseling from Spring Arbor University.

I bring much life experience into the counseling environment, providing treatment for anxiety, depression, and life changes, with a particular focus on trauma, grief and loss, conflict, and anger. I use a variety of therapeutic approaches including EMDR, Internal Family Systems, Emotionally Focused Therapy for couples and individuals, Cognitive Behavioral Therapy, and somatic and mindfulness awareness, through a trauma-informed lens.

Kelli Smith

Licensed Professional Counselor, EMDR Trained, EFT Trained, IFS Informed, CSAT Candidate, Schema Therapy Informed

Sometimes the waves of life can become overwhelming, and the undertow of trauma has you feeling like you are losing your footing and life seems unmanageable. Counseling is a transformative experience where you receive insight and have compassion for yourself. You are courageous to embark on this journey. You are enough.

I provide therapy for various issues, including anxiety, depression, PTSD/trauma, sex addiction, love addiction, enmeshment, and grief. Traumatic events and childhood trauma can keep someone feeling stuck and trapped. I use various therapeutic approaches, including psychodynamic and attachment-based approaches with a trauma-informed lens; this includes Emotionally Focused Therapy (EFT) for individuals, Internal Family Systems (IFS), and Eye Movement Desensitization and Reprocessing (EMDR).

I work with those who struggle with an addiction to pornography or other sexual behaviors, and I work with partners who feel betrayed by these behaviors. I have training from the Association of Partners of Sex Addicts Trauma Specialists (APSATS) to work with partners of sex addicts, and I am a Certified Sex Addiction Therapist (CSAT) Candidate.

I graduated with my bachelor’s in education and have been a teacher for many years. I felt called into counseling and received my Master of Arts in Clinical Mental Health Counseling from the Townsend Institute for Leadership and Counseling at Concordia University Irvine. I can incorporate faith-based counseling if you choose.

You are the expert in your life, and parts of us can become unbalanced; together, we can make realistic boundaries and reasonable goals so that you can heal and grow.

Stacy Quick

LPC, LPC Supervisor, EMDR Trained, TF-CBT (Trauma-Focused Cognitive Behavioral Therapy), ERP (Exposure and Response Prevention) Trained

As a compassionate and experienced therapist, I help individuals and families navigate life transitions and relationship challenges.

I specialize in treating Obsessive Compulsive Disorder (OCD) and have received advanced training in Exposure and Response Prevention (ERP) from leading specialists in the field. I have had the privilege of supporting clients from around the world as they pursue healing and recovery, particularly those navigating the complexities of OCD and trauma.

My areas of focus also include Post-Traumatic Stress Disorder (PTSD), generalized anxiety, separation anxiety, social anxiety, depression, and trauma. I work with individuals facing circumstances that may feel overwhelming or beyond their control. I am trained in EMDR and informed by Internal Family Systems (IFS), allowing me to take a thoughtful, integrative approach to care.

I work with children ages 8 and older, adolescents, and adults. For clients who desire it, I offer Christian-based counseling, integrating faith into the therapeutic process in a thoughtful and respectful way. At the same time, I am committed to providing high-quality, inclusive care to individuals of all beliefs and backgrounds.

My mission is simple: to help the hurting, foster healing, and walk alongside individuals and families as they face life’s challenges with courage and hope.

I provide client-centered, trauma-informed care in a safe and compassionate environment where clients feel heard, respected, and supported. I enjoy working with individuals experiencing anxiety, depression, trauma, grief, relationship concerns, life transitions, and emotional regulation challenges. I value lifelong learning and pursue ongoing professional development to provide high-quality care.

It is a privilege to walk alongside clients as they discover hope, build resilience, and move toward greater healing, peace, purpose, and wholeness.

David Harrington

Master of Social Work Student Intern, EMDR Trained, CBT (Cognitive Behavioral Therapy)

I like to believe that when we are born, we are given a backpack that we carry our whole lives. As we go through life, we accumulate bricks in this backpack. These bricks are negative or painful events we experience over our lives. Whether loved ones, life circumstances, or strangers place those bricks in it, sometimes we don’t even realize how heavy that backpack gets. Some of us have carried the weight of so many bricks that we don’t know if we can take another step.

You don’t have to carry that weight alone. Relief and rest are possible. I believe that at times we all need someone to come alongside and help carry some of the weight, and sometimes we need help taking those bricks out of our backpacks. It takes courage and strength to say “I need help.” Whether the weight you’re carrying is anxiety, depression, trauma, grief, or addiction, I will walk with you and help carry that weight.

I realized my passion was to help people while serving with adolescent youth as a leader at a church youth group and as a camp counselor. I received my Bachelor’s in Human Services from Grace Christian University, and I will complete my Master of Social Work in December 2026. While my primary training is in Cognitive Behavioral Therapy (CBT), I am receiving training in EMDR, Internal Family Systems (IFS), and Emotionally Focused Therapy (EFT).

Good Faith Estimate

You have the right to receive a “Good Faith Estimate” explaining how much your medical care will cost. Under the law, health care providers need to give patients who don’t have insurance or who are not using insurance an estimate of the bill for medical items and services. You have the right to receive a Good Faith Estimate for the total expected cost of any non-emergency items or services. Make sure your health care provider gives you a Good Faith Estimate in writing at least 1 business day before your medical service or item. You can also ask your healthcare provider, and any other provider you choose, for a Good Faith Estimate before you schedule an item or service. Make sure to save a copy or picture of your Good Faith Estimate. For questions or more information about your right to a Good Faith Estimate, visit www.cms.gov/nosurprises or call (631) 332-2213.
