About Us

Who We Are | Still Water Professional Counseling

Life is a journey with its ups and downs.  The joys and the memories of the good times are precious, but the pain of the disappointments and betrayals often endure just as strongly or even stronger than the memories of the joyful times.  Too often we suffer alone, trying to find something that will take away our pain or help us to forget.  Problems can arise like depression or anxiety, or addictive behaviors can develop which take control of our lives and make it unmanageable. Marriages and relationships encounter trials which can seem insurmountable.  Sometimes, by ourselves, we are simply unable to find the peace we want to experience.

At Still Water Professional Counseling we provide a compassionate and caring environment to help people deal with the issues of life.

Still Water Professional Counseling is committed to providing quality individualized treatment that respects each person’s spiritual and personal values.   However, we specialize in counseling that integrates a biblical worldview with research based psychological treatment.

Good Faith Estimate

You have the right to receive a “Good Faith Estimate” explaining how much your medical care will cost. Under the law, health care providers need to give patients who don’t have insurance or who are not using insurance an estimate of the bill for medical items and services. You have the right to receive a Good Faith Estimate for the total expected cost of any non-emergency items or services. Make sure your health care provider gives you a Good Faith Estimate in writing at least 1 business day before your medical service or item. You can also ask your healthcare provider, and any other provider you choose, for a Good Faith Estimate before you schedule an item or service. Make sure to save a copy or picture of your Good Faith Estimate. For questions or more information about your right to a Good Faith Estimate, visit www.cms.gov/nosurprises or call (631) 332-2213.
