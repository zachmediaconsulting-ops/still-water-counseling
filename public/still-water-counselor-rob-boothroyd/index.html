Our Counselors

Rob Boothroyd

Licensed Professional Counselor, CSAT (Certified Sexual Addiction Therapist), EMDR Trained, EFT Trained, IFS Level 1 Trained

Today, many people struggle with sexually compulsive behaviors. They often tell themselves that it is normal, while being secretive about what they are doing, and they are unable to stop even when they sincerely try. I am a Certified Sex Addiction Therapist (CSAT), and I completed this extensive training to help people find freedom from compulsive sexual practices.

Freedom is possible. Because many of my clients are married, I use EFT (Emotionally Focused Therapy) to help them heal the relational wounds caused by the addiction. Additionally, because many people have unresolved trauma, I have trained in EMDR to help them deal with the wounds of the past.

I earned a Bachelor of Arts degree from the University of Delaware and a Professional Counseling degree from Liberty University. My working experience includes 10 years as a full-time staff member of Campus Crusade for Christ (Cru) and 20 years in the staffing industry.

Good Faith Estimate

You have the right to receive a “Good Faith Estimate” explaining how much your medical care will cost. Under the law, health care providers need to give patients who don’t have insurance or who are not using insurance an estimate of the bill for medical items and services. You have the right to receive a Good Faith Estimate for the total expected cost of any non-emergency items or services. Make sure your health care provider gives you a Good Faith Estimate in writing at least 1 business day before your medical service or item. You can also ask your healthcare provider, and any other provider you choose, for a Good Faith Estimate before you schedule an item or service. Make sure to save a copy or picture of your Good Faith Estimate. For questions or more information about your right to a Good Faith Estimate, visit www.cms.gov/nosurprises or call (631) 332-2213.
